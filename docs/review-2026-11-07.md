## Changes

- Replace fixed 24-order selection with `adaptive_mass_v1`: order tickets by their pre-race model probabilities; select the smallest prefix covering 70% of the probability mass conditional on all three finishers being in the chosen five, subject to a floor of six and ceiling of 36 orders. Three- and four-horse races use the available permutations within these limits.
- Keep the existing top-five trio BOX. The five-horse selection and probability model are unchanged. This release adjusts stake allocation and diagnostics, not horse-rating weights based on a single day's outcomes.
- Display actual ticket count, hypothetical stake, concentration label, conditional coverage, absolute model coverage and a warning when the candidate-five mass is below 40%. Explicitly disclose when the 36-order cap falls short of the 70% conditional target. None of these model probabilities is an empirically calibrated hit-rate promise.
- Store policy metadata with new forecasts in both NAR and licensed JRA pipelines and manual/feed snapshots. Identify NAR new forecasts as v5 to distinguish their future results from v4.
- Add a filter-aware live review and per-race loss classification. Preserve old forecasts, tickets, payouts and hit judgments; existing drafts frozen under the old policy keep their saved picks.
